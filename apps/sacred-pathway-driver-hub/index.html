FAQ

Questions Google can index.

Is Driver Hub a load board?

No. Driver Hub is positioned as a trucking operations, paperwork, expense, and paystub app. It complements load boards by helping drivers manage the load after it is booked.

Who is Sacred Pathway Driver Hub for?

It is built for truck drivers, owner-operators, and small carriers who need a practical way to track loads, receipts, broker records, expenses, documents, and pay.

Can the app scan rate confirmations and receipts?

Yes. Public App Store metadata describes Smart Scan support for rate confirmations, BOLs, receipts, and editable parsed fields.

Does Driver Hub help with driver paystubs?

Yes. It can create professional paystub exports based on selected loads, expenses, date ranges, and configurable pay or deduction rules.
