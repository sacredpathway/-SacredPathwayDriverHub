FAQ

Questions Google can index.

Is Sacred Bill Tracker a budgeting app or a bill reminder app?

It is primarily a bill reminder and bill organizer app. It helps users track due dates, recurring bills, subscriptions, business bills, weekly income, and reports without requiring a bank connection.

Can Sacred Bill Tracker separate household and business bills?

Yes. The app is positioned around household and business organization so users can see personal bills, business bills, or everything together.

Does Sacred Bill Tracker require bank linking?

No. The app is designed for manual organization and privacy-conscious tracking without connecting a bank account.

Who should use Sacred Bill Tracker?

It fits households, freelancers, small business owners, and owner-operators who need a simple recurring bill tracker, due date calendar, and exportable bill records.
