Trucking app vs spreadsheet: tracking loads, expenses, and paperwork

A spreadsheet can calculate totals, but it does not naturally scan rate confirmations, store load paperwork, attach receipts, or export driver paystubs from a mobile workflow.

View Sacred Pathway Driver Hub Open App Store

Decision guide

  • Use a spreadsheet when you need a custom ledger and can maintain it after every load.
  • Use Driver Hub when the job is mobile paperwork capture, load status, expense tracking, broker records, and paystub exports.
  • The strongest SEO angle is not replacing every TMS; it is replacing scattered load records for owner-operators and small carriers.
