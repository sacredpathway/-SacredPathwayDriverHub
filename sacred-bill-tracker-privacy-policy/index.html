Sacred Pathway LLC ("we", "our", "us") operates the Sacred Bill Tracker mobile application and related customer support workflows. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and protect information when you use Sacred Bill Tracker or contact us for app support.

1. Information We Collect

Account Information: When you create or sign in to a Sacred Bill Tracker account, we may collect the email address associated with your account, authentication information needed to keep you signed in, and user account identifiers assigned by our authentication provider.

Bill and Financial Organization Data: Sacred Bill Tracker lets you enter bills, due dates, recurring payments, subscriptions, payment status, categories, notes, financial profiles, reports, exports, and related organization details. We process this information so the app can provide bill tracking, reminders, calendar views, dashboards, reports, and exports.

Support Requests: If you contact support by email, social media, Meta Page engagement, or another support channel, we may process your message, sender-scoped support identifier, issue details, device type, app version, timestamps, and support status so we can respond and troubleshoot.

Meta Support Workflow Data: For the Sacred Bill Tracker Meta support workflow, we use official Meta tools and webhooks for Facebook Page support review. The current workflow is limited to Facebook Page connection and Page engagement intake for customer support. We process Meta webhook event identifiers, sender-scoped Meta IDs, Page engagement metadata, support issue details voluntarily sent by the user, and operational logs such as channel, event ID, intent, confidence, and text length. We do not ask users to send passwords, full card numbers, bank login details, Social Security numbers, or private payment credentials through Meta messages or comments.

Technical App Data: We may process basic technical information needed to operate, secure, troubleshoot, and maintain the app, website, and support systems.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to provide and maintain Sacred Bill Tracker, including account sign-in, bill tracking, due date reminders, subscription tracking, calendar views, reports, exports, support responses, abuse prevention, security, reliability, and privacy requests.

For the Meta support workflow, we use information only to support user-initiated customer support interactions, review Page engagement, route issues to a human support workflow, prevent duplicate processing, rate-limit abuse, and demonstrate compliance for Meta App Review.

We do not use Sacred Bill Tracker data or Meta support workflow data for third-party advertising. We do not sell personal information. We do not use cross-app tracking.

3. Data Sharing

We share information only with service providers needed to operate and support the app, such as app hosting, authentication, database, email, Apple platform services, Meta platform services for user-initiated support workflows, and operational infrastructure providers. These providers may process data only as needed to provide their services to us.

We do not sell, trade, rent, scrape, or export user data to advertisers, data brokers, competitors, or unrelated third parties.

4. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards designed to protect information, including encrypted transmission, authenticated access, secure backend services, token encryption for connected support assets, and access controls. No method of transmission or electronic storage is completely secure, but we work to limit access to what is needed for app functionality and support.

5. Meta Support Workflow

The Sacred Bill Tracker Meta support workflow is for customer support and App Review demonstration. It is not used for marketing spam, bulk outreach, scraping, unofficial APIs, or contacting users who did not initiate an eligible interaction.

The current Meta App Review scope is limited to Facebook Page support workflows that use pages_show_list, pages_manage_metadata, and pages_read_engagement. Messenger, Instagram messaging, Instagram comments, and Facebook comment-reply automation are deferred unless and until the related permissions are reviewed, approved, and enabled.

Human replies and outbound support actions remain blocked until required permissions are approved, outbound messaging is intentionally enabled, and the relevant support channel settings allow the action.

6. Data Retention and Account Deletion

We retain information for as long as your account remains active or as long as needed to provide the app, maintain security, comply with legal obligations, resolve disputes, prevent abuse, and support users.

You may delete your Sacred Bill Tracker account from inside the app where account deletion is available. If you cannot access the in-app delete option, email sacredbilltracker@gmail.com with "Sacred Bill Tracker account deletion" in the subject line and the email address connected to the account. We will help verify the request and complete deletion.

When your account is deleted, we delete or deactivate account data associated with your Sacred Bill Tracker account, subject to any limited retention required for security, backups, legal compliance, fraud prevention, or dispute resolution.

7. Children's Privacy

Sacred Bill Tracker is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us and we will take appropriate steps to delete it.

8. Your Rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or request information about the personal data we maintain about you. You can manage your account in the app or contact us at sacredbilltracker@gmail.com for privacy requests.

9. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised effective date.

10. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, the Meta support workflow, or a privacy request, contact Sacred Pathway LLC at sacredbilltracker@gmail.com.
